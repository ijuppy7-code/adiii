Chapter One

The summit and that first hello

You know what still makes me laugh? At the summit, I was pretty sure you were ignoring me 😂 And then you were the one who said hello first. I don’t think you knew what to make of me back then. To be fair, I had no idea what to expect from you either. But the more we talked, the more comfortable we got around each other. Somewhere along the way, you became the person I wanted to tell things to, even when nothing interesting had happened. If you’d told me at that summit that I’d end up this close to you, I would’ve asked, “Her? The one who’s ignoring me?” And now look at us 😂❤️

Chapter Two

The reel that started it

By then, our weekend calls had already become something I looked forward to. And that day, I was just scrolling through reels and asking you whatever random questions came up. You kept going, “Pass, pass,” and every time you said it, I got a little more curious 😂

I don’t even remember which question got us there, but somehow we went from laughing over a reel to having a proper conversation about how we felt. I was nervous to tell you I liked you. And then you told me you liked me too, and there I was smiling at my phone like an idiot 😂❤️

I still don’t know what I’d call what we felt back then. I just know I really liked you, and it felt good to hear you say you felt the same. We gave it a shot for a week, and I’ll always remember how it started. Of all things, one random reel and you going “pass, pass.” 🫂

Chapter Three

Us, after that

My favourite part of our story might actually be what happened after that week. We still called, still told each other things, and somehow got even closer. Now you’ll flirt with me, I’ll flirt back, and five minutes later I’ll have irritated you enough to get that “okay, enough” voice 😂 That’s usually my cue to behave, which I manage for about two minutes.

But when something’s actually bothering me, you’re also one of the few people I feel comfortable calling and saying, “Hey, can we talk?” I hope you know you can call me like that too. I’m really glad we can be idiots together and still show up for each other when it matters. I love that about our friendship. 🫂

Happy birthday. ❤️ I’m really, really glad you said hello.
